Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Overview

is a referral platform that helps homeowners request connections with independent local pest control providers. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when visitors use this website.

Information collected

Information may include contact details, service-location details, pest concern descriptions, device data, browser data, pages visited, referral source, and communications submitted through phone, form, or email interactions.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, route requests to independent providers, maintain website functionality, analyze performance, prevent misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Sharing with providers

When a homeowner requests a connection, relevant inquiry details may be shared with independent local providers so they can contact the homeowner about availability, estimates, and service terms. Providers are independent businesses with their own privacy practices.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use cookies, analytics tools, call tracking, and similar technologies to understand website performance and improve user experience. Cookie details are described in the Cookie Policy.

Choices

Visitors may avoid submitting forms, disable cookies through browser settings, or contact the site using the email listed in the footer for privacy-related questions.
