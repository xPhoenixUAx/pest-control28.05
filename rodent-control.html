Overview

Rodent concerns often involve both activity and access.

A provider conversation may include droppings, nesting areas, garage gaps, attic access, utility penetrations, and food storage conditions. Some providers separate trapping, monitoring, sanitation guidance, and exclusion work into different scopes.

PestLine Connect does not perform inspections or make repair recommendations. Homeowners should review the provider's written findings and decide independently.

Common situations

  • Scratching or movement sounds in walls, ceilings, or attic spaces.
  • Droppings near cabinets, storage shelves, or garage edges.
  • Gnaw marks around food storage, insulation, or utility lines.
  • Visible gaps around doors, vents, rooflines, or foundation areas.

Questions to ask

  • Will the provider identify possible entry points?
  • Are exclusion repairs included in the estimate?
  • What monitoring and follow-up schedule is proposed?
