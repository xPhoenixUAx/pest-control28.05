Overview

Flea and tick conversations should consider indoor, outdoor, and pet-related factors.

Homeowners may notice bites, pet scratching, activity after yard exposure, or concerns following wildlife contact. Independent providers can explain how they evaluate living areas, yard edges, shaded zones, and preparation requirements.

PestLine Connect does not provide veterinary advice or perform pest control work. It helps homeowners connect with independent local companies.

Common situations

  • Bites around ankles or lower legs.
  • Pets scratching more than usual or recent pet exposure.
  • Activity after yard, trail, or wildlife contact.
  • Concerns in rugs, upholstery, bedding, or shaded outdoor areas.

Questions to ask

  • How should pet care be coordinated?
  • What indoor and outdoor areas are reviewed?
  • What written preparation and re-entry instructions are provided?
