Overview

Ant concerns often begin outside before becoming visible indoors.

Homeowners may notice ant activity around kitchen counters, windows, bathrooms, patios, slab edges, or landscaping. A local provider may ask about where trails appear, when activity increases, and whether moisture or food access could be contributing to the issue.

PestLine Connect does not identify species or perform treatments. It helps start a conversation with independent local providers who can explain their own inspection process and available options.

Common situations

  • Ant trails near food preparation areas or sinks.
  • Activity returning after cleaning, sealing, or store-bought products.
  • Small soil piles or nesting signs around exterior edges.
  • Seasonal movement after rain, heat, or landscape changes.

Questions to ask

  • What ant species may be involved and why does that matter?
  • Will interior and exterior conditions both be reviewed?
  • What preparation, follow-up, or prevention steps will be included in writing?

Provider conversation

Ask how the provider evaluates entry points, nesting conditions, and recurrence risk. Confirm pricing and service terms directly.
