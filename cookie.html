1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how ("we," "us," or "our") may use cookies and similar tracking technologies on this website. This policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

By using this website, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree to our use of cookies, you may adjust your browser settings or choose not to use this website.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, remember preferences, and provide information to website owners.

Cookies may include session cookies that expire when you close your browser, persistent cookies that remain for a set period or until deleted, first-party cookies set by this website, and third-party cookies set by another domain.

3. Types of cookies we may use

Essential cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They may support security, network management, accessibility, form functionality, cookie consent preferences, and basic session behavior. You cannot opt out of cookies that are strictly necessary for core site operation.

Analytics cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the website by collecting and reporting information such as traffic, page views, referral sources, and performance signals. This helps us improve site usability and reliability.

Functional cookies

These cookies may enable enhanced functionality, such as remembering preferences or temporarily supporting form interactions. If disabled, certain website features may not work as intended.

Marketing, referral, and call-tracking cookies

These cookies may help us understand how visitors reach the website, measure advertising or referral performance, support call tracking, and improve homeowner connection workflows. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose tools are used on the site.

4. Third-party cookies

We may use third-party services that set cookies or similar technologies on your device, such as analytics providers, advertising platforms, call-tracking tools, spam-prevention tools, and social media or referral measurement services. These third parties may collect information about your online activity across different websites according to their own policies.

We do not control third-party cookies. You should review the privacy and cookie policies of any third-party services for details about their practices and controls.

5. How to manage cookies

You have the ability to accept, reject, block, or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers accept cookies automatically, but you can usually modify settings to decline or remove cookies.

Common browser controls are available in Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, and Microsoft Edge settings. You may also use available opt-out tools from certain analytics or advertising providers, such as Google Analytics opt-out tools, the Digital Advertising Alliance, or the Network Advertising Initiative.

Blocking or deleting cookies may affect your experience on this website. Some features, forms, referral tracking, call tracking, or performance measurement may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

6. Do Not Track signals

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" feature. Because there is no consistent industry standard for how Do Not Track signals should be interpreted, this website does not currently respond to Do Not Track browser signals or similar mechanisms.

7. Updates to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. When updates are made, the revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
