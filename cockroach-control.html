Overview

Roach concerns often require a practical look at conditions around the home.

Homeowners may notice activity at night, droppings near appliances, or recurring sightings around moisture sources. Providers may discuss sanitation, exclusion, monitoring, and service intervals depending on the situation.

Because providers are independent, homeowners should compare written estimates and verify credentials before choosing a company.

Common situations

  • Activity near sinks, appliances, cabinets, or utility rooms.
  • Droppings, egg cases, or odors in hidden spaces.
  • Recurring activity in apartments, condos, or shared-wall homes.
  • Moisture or storage conditions that may contribute to activity.

Questions to ask

  • What areas will be inspected?
  • How are sanitation and exclusion recommendations documented?
  • What service intervals, fees, and follow-up terms are included?

Written plan

Request a clear explanation of what is included, what is excluded, and what the homeowner is expected to do before or after service.
