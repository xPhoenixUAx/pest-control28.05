Overview

Termite inspection requests should be specific about timing and documentation.

Homeowners may request termite inspection conversations because of visible signs, recurring moisture issues, or real estate transaction needs. Provider requirements can vary by state, property type, and report purpose.

PestLine Connect does not issue inspection reports. Any findings, estimates, or recommendations come from the independent provider selected by the homeowner.

Common situations

  • Mud tubes, soft wood, damaged trim, or bubbling paint.
  • Swarmers or discarded wings near windows and lights.
  • Real estate closing, refinancing, or documentation needs.
  • Moisture or wood-to-soil contact concerns around the structure.

Questions to ask

  • What license applies to termite inspection in the service area?
  • What report will be delivered and when?
  • Does the estimate include inspection only or additional service proposals?
